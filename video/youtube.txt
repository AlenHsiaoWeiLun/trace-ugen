DESCRIPTION
TRACE is an edge copilot for the station duty officer. It fuses camera detections, alarm-panel signals and staff radio calls into one evidence-linked incident card, and wakes an on-device vision-language model (Qwen2-VL-2B) only when an incident needs a second opinion. Target hardware: ASUS UGen300 (Hailo-10H, 2.5 W) — YOLO, Whisper, Qwen2-VL and a 1.5B LLM on one stick; raw video and audio never leave the box.
