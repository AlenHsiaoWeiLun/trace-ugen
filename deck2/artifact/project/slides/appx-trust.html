Appendix B · Trust and privacy

What VESTIGE will not do.

No unauthorized sources

Only cameras and records a workspace is permitted to use. No covert monitoring, no assumed access to campus or public CCTV.

No identities

No face recognition by default. People are pseudonymous tracks; head regions are blurred in stored evidence.

No accusations

Movement is not theft; proximity is not intent. VESTIGE describes observations; people decide responsibility.

No filled gaps

Missing footage stays missing. Synthetic data is labelled and never merged with real observations.

Edge by default

Raw video and audio stay on site; metadata and selected clips are kept for a configurable retention period, then deleted.

Every action audited

Model verdicts and human decisions land in one audit log, aligned with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act.
