02 · At city scale

When it matters most, the evidence is scattered.

19 DEC 2025

An attack begins between exits M7 and M8 of Taipei Main Station and continues to Zhongshan.

B2 LEVEL

40-odd cameras on 4 recording hosts. One host's disk was damaged: 7 cameras could be watched live but not replayed.

AFTER

Investigators rebuilt the route by cross-referencing 12 other cameras, floor by floor.

Investigation means stitching evidence across cameras — and knowing exactly what is missing.

Source: CNA 2025-12-22 (Taipei Metro briefing); CNA topic 4863

Illustration, not the station

Taipei Metro stated no camera malfunctioned; one recording host's disk was damaged so its 7 cameras could not replay. We do not claim VESTIGE would have prevented the attack — only that investigation is cross-camera evidence work, and gaps must be explicit.
