Why Lightning, not Thunderstorm. The workload is event-driven: cheap detection always, 2B-class models rarely. It fits one 2.5 W stick; a 290 W GPU would mostly idle.

One chip, shared. All models share one HailoRT VDevice. Target: 8 cameras at 15 fps ≈ 40% of published YOLO11n capacity.

Not yet measured on a UGen300 — USB throughput, power and multi-model contention are the first Stage II tests.
