The gap

The digital world is searchable. The physical world isn't.

Search your inbox

"invoice from March" → the answer, in under a second.

Search the world around you

"where did my bike go?" → hours of footage, many camera owners, no index.

Cameras record pixels. Nobody records what happened.

The thesis. We search documents instantly; we cannot search what happened in a place. Video is stored as pixels, not as memory of events.
