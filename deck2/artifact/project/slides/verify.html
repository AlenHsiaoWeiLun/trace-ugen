Know where the AI fails — and what helps.

33%→62%

Under partial occlusion the detector keeps a third of what it finds on clean frames. In this test a larger model raised that to 62% — at 75 vs 46 ms per frame.

And what hurts: naive low-light brightening lowered retention from 90% to 79%. We measure before we trust.

24 frames of Taipei Bridge traffic · 98 reference detections · laptop benchmark, 11 Oct 2026. Retention = share of the model's own clean-frame detections still found (IoU ≥ 0.5), not accuracy against human labels.
