Go-to-market

Our first market: construction.

WHY HERE FIRST

Cameras are already on site, hazards are physical and recurring, and every serious incident needs an investigation. Regulators are moving: Hong Kong now requires a Smart Site Safety System on public works above HK$30M.

WHO

Buyer: safety (EHS) and operations leadership. Daily users: site managers and safety officers who must answer "what happened, and why was it flagged?"

VALIDATION SIGNAL

Voxel, Protex AI and Intenseye sell vision safety; TSMC uses AI CCTV on its own construction site. The need is proven; edge-native, evidence-graded investigation is our opening.
